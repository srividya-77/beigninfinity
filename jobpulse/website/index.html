How it works

01

Connect your accounts

Sign in with each of your job-hunting Gmail accounts via Google OAuth. JobPulse securely stores your tokens server-side — never in the browser.

→
02

We watch in real time

Using Gmail's push notification system (Pub/Sub), your Node backend receives an alert within seconds of any new email — no polling, no delays.

→
03

Only what matters surfaces

Emails are classified instantly. Shortlist signals fire a desktop alert. Rejections update your board silently. Noise never reaches you.

Built for the serious job hunt

📡

Real-time push alerts

Gmail Pub/Sub delivers email events in seconds. Not every 30 seconds. Not every minute. Seconds.

∞

No account limit

Add as many Gmail accounts as you use for job hunting. The dashboard scales to fit your workflow, not the other way around.

🎯

Intelligent filtering

Precision keyword engine separates shortlist signals from noise. Interview invitations and assessments get alerts. Everything else gets logged.

🤫

Silent rejection logging

Rejections update your board without a notification. Your attention and emotional energy are treated as the limited resource they are.

✍️

Passive application capture

Type your monitored email into a Greenhouse or Lever form — JobPulse silently logs the company and role. Zero manual entry.

🔒

Privacy-first

OAuth tokens live on the server, never in the extension. Your email content is classified and discarded — never stored.
